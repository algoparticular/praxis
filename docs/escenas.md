# Escenas de las cartas

Detalle de cómo está construida cada escena inmersiva. Estaba en `CLAUDE.md` y se movió acá, sin cambios y en inglés, para que `CLAUDE.md` quede corto. Qué hace cada escena al tocarla: `registro-cartas-vivas.md`. Qué propone cada verbo: `verbos-y-dinamicas.md`.

## Paciencia (27) y Vipassana (31)

`ScenePaciencia` (id 27) and `SceneVipassana` (id 31) are built from separate layers that move with depth (pointer / tilt / scroll parallax via `useParallax.js`, plus each layer's own idle motion). Vipassana reuses the landing hero: `useCellField`, `useHeroSpiral` and `useCharacterLife`. Vipassana's verb is Soltar (let go) and the scene has a goal: the field holds 21 cells (15 on low-power devices), each one something to let go of. A press on a cell lets go of it: it bursts into dots that stay gone (`cellField.popCell()`, `release` sound through `onEvent`). The dotted spiral at his third eye is the count and it gets shorter with every cell that goes (`spiral.progress()`); if nothing is touched for a while one of the cells that are left glows now and then (`cellField.hint()`, no words). A press on the water is one breath of the whole scene (`breathCurve.js`): `cellField.ripple()` sends a ring through the water (a shader plane) that shoves the cells (its strength follows how long the press was held); when the ring reaches the character `life.inhale()` and `cellField.breathe()` draw everything in towards him (`breath` sound) and as he lets it out his spiral lights up (`onExhale`). It is the rhythm of the scene and is not needed to finish it. Holding the character himself is holding his breath in, which is clinging (`inhale` sound): his spiral shortens (`spiral.hold()`, 3.2s) until it is a point at his third eye, the field leans in and the cells nearest to him are caught one after the other, a third of them (`CATCH` in `useCellField.js`: 7 of 21), so three held breaths are enough to let go of all of them; held a moment longer, the caught cells and the matter orbit him like electrons round a nucleus, a cosmic mandala (`orbitW` in `useCellField.js`): six orbits crossing at him, 30 degrees apart (three small quick ones between three wide slow ones, turning in alternate directions), with the orbits staying put and Sid rising as they form (`RISE` in `breathCurve.js`: 17% of his own height up and 7% of his width to the left (`SIDE`: 4% by measure plus what the lean towards the finger and the scene's depth parallax take), so that the mass of his body, which is not at the middle of his picture, ends in the middle of the orbits; `getRise` in `useCharacterLife.js`) and going back to where he was on release, the enormous cells on an orbit shrinking while it lasts (`ORBIT_CELL_MAX`; the small ones keep their size) so that he is what matters, and with the electrons evenly spaced along each and coming forward (bigger, brighter) and going back (smaller, dimmer) as they go round; the orbits themselves are never drawn, they are only in how things move; and on release (`exhale` sound) the spiral comes back out shining, the big ring runs from him (`ripple(x, y, 1, impact)` throws cells and matter much harder) and `cellField.letGoCaught()` lets go of every cell he had caught as the ring reaches it. Easter egg, not announced: held far longer (`POWER_AT` in `useCellField.js`, 4.2s at the top of the orbit) every cell left joins the orbits (`powered()`, `power` event: `star` sound), and on release the ring is harder and faster and they all break together (`sceneMilestone('power_breath')`). Whether the finger is on Sid is read from a precomputed mask (`characterMask.js`, made by `scripts/make-character-mask.mjs`; reading a canvas at runtime fails in desktop Safari), and the scene stops the browser's own press behaviours on him (selection, drag, Force Touch lookup). When no cell is left (`startWin` in the scene) the dotted spiral goes (`spiral.fade()`) and the rulito (`Rulito.vue`, in the pink of the spiral, small and floating clear of his head) is drawn, the card's name lights up and the `rulito` sounds; then `cellField.gather()` brings every cell back (`bloom` sounds) as the rulito's tail catches up with its head, and the spiral comes back (`sceneMilestone('all_released')`; the idea is in docs/verbos-y-dinamicas.md). Reduced motion keeps the scene static: it cannot be played yet. Paciencia is the first card with a verb as a game (Arraigar: the pine cone is held, a network of golden roots (`useRoots.js`, a canvas over the mushrooms that stays once grown) spreads under the field, its charge fills, and a rulito over the central Sama's head, drawn in code on a canvas by `Rulito.vue`, is born as a dot, stretches into a line and curls into its loop, and when let go its tail catches up with its head until it is gone; see `docs/verbos-y-dinamicas.md`). Its boat is `boat-bare.webp` (the rulito removed); `boat.webp` keeps its rulito for the Collaborate page.

**The WebGL stage (every scene).** Since the pilot in Paciencia, the scenes are drawn whole in WebGL by `useLayerStage.js`: one canvas and one renderer per scene, every picture a plane at its depth, and the camera slides with the pointer or the tilt of the phone (`stage.look`) with its lens shifted so the focal plane stays put, which is the parallax: each part moves as far as it did in the DOM version (`amp / (SLIDE + amp)` gives its depth). Everything is laid out in px of the screen at rest, computed from the CSS of the DOM version (`layout()` or `place()` in each scene). The particles of `useStardust.js` are drawn among the layers (`{ stage, groups: [{ order }] }`), and can follow a layer (`shift` with `stage.drift(z)`) or turn with it (`turn`). Touch targets are DOM elements moved over what they belong to every frame (`boxOnScreen`, `toScreen`), so they still take a finger and the keyboard. Shader effects: `wind` (Paciencia's grass, Evolución's fronds, Pañña's leaves), `shimmer` (the air over Paciencia's sun, the organelles seen through the water, Sincronicidad's bubbles, Vipassana's rays), `water` (the light of the water across the inside of Hogar, Creatividad and Evolución, and Khanti's pink: a net of thin bright lines, `CAUSTIC`; broad soft bands were tried first and blurred the parts of the cells), `sheen` (a band of light over Khanti's glass), `sink` (Khanti's flasks empty in the shader), `blur`, `vignette`, `clip` and mirrors (`sx`/`sy`, Pañña's forest), discs and glows drawn in code. Canvases drawn by a scene (the rulito, Paciencia's roots, Creatividad's and Evolución's interiors, Khanti's thread, Pañña's floor) are shown as textures; when such a canvas changes size (a resize, a phone turned) `refresh()` makes its texture again, since a texture keeps the size it was first uploaded with. A scene builds its layers and registers `place()` before it starts the particle groups measured on them, so on a resize they are made again after the layout. Bloom only while `stage.bloom` is above zero: the rulito moments of Paciencia, Hogar, Dana, Pañña, Vipassana, Evolución, Khanti and Sincronicidad (and, softer, while its bubbles are lit), with the same value as `--name-glow`. Vipassana draws its field of cells with its own three.js scene and camera in the stage's renderer (`useVipassanaField.js` through `stage.underlay()`, a copy of the landing's `useCellField.js`, which stays as it was); Sid is drawn on the focal plane so his hidden element (moved by `useCharacterLife`, which hands the stage each frame with `onFrame`) is exactly where he is seen, and his glow is his own picture blurred and tinted in the shader instead of a CSS drop-shadow. Without WebGL a card shows its flat illustration (`stage()` in `cardScenes.js`). The tilt on iOS needs a permission that is asked once in the life of the app, on the first touch of a visit (`useTilt.js`, remembered in `localStorage`); without it the parallax follows the finger.

**Paciencia in WebGL (the pilot).** Paciencia is drawn whole in WebGL by `useLayerStage.js`: one canvas and one renderer for the scene, every picture a plane at its depth, and the camera slides with the pointer or the tilt of the phone (`stage.look`) with its lens shifted so the focal plane stays put, which is the parallax (the sun moves 8px at the edge of a swipe, the boat 22, the ground 40; `depthFor` in the scene). The layout is the one of the DOM version, computed in `layout()` in px of the screen; on a phone the strip of coral and mushrooms is 30% bigger, so the pine cone sits higher. Shader effects: the grass sways in the wind (`wind`), the air trembles over the sun (`shimmer`), which is also a little out of focus (`blur`) and has a soft light that breathes (`glow`); the pine cone is lit by its material (`bright`, `saturate`) and a halo, not by CSS filters. The spores (`useSpores.js` with `points: true`) are particle groups of `useStardust.js` drawn by the stage between the boat and the grass, and the gold sparks in front; the roots and the rulito are their 2D canvases shown as textures. The touch target of the pine cone is a DOM element moved over it every frame (`boxOnScreen`), so it still takes a finger and the keyboard; it has `touch-action: none`, so a finger that drifts a little while holding is not taken for a scroll that lets go of the hold. When the rulito is completed the stage blooms (`stage.bloom`, the same value as `--name-glow`): the rulito, the cone, its halo, the roots and the sparks are drawn again small, blurred and added on top; it only runs while it is above zero. Without WebGL the card shows its flat illustration (`stage()` in `cardScenes.js`). The tilt on iOS needs a permission that is asked once in the life of the app, on the first touch of a visit (`useTilt.js`, remembered in `localStorage`); without it the parallax follows the finger.
## Nekkhamma (0)

`SceneNekkhamma` (0) is built from the layers in `public/assets/nekkhamma` and `nekkhammaScene.json`, both made by `scripts/convert-nekkhamma.mjs` from the exported PNGs (each picture cropped to its box, in px of the 2160 square; and every dot of the layers that are fields of dots, as [x, y, radius, colour]: the stars, the pink specks, Sid's dots and the cosmic cloud, which are all particles). The sky is a CSS gradient in the card's colour; the wind strokes, the planet (its ring a separate picture), the cloud with its two little galaxies (turning) and a soft glow, the ground (stretched to both sides of the screen) with its plants, buds and the mark, and Sid are planes at their depths. The illustration draws four Sids, each more undone than the last; the scene has one, who goes through those four states (`STATES`, each a group of pictures plus its dots in the `body` particle group), standing where each is drawn (`X`, his head in art px) and walking from one to the next (`pos`). Its verb is Soltar: a touch lets go of a state (`forward()`): its dots (or, from the whole Sid, the dots his legs will become) fly to random dots of the cloud along curves, taking their colours on the way (`flyers`, a `dynamic` group of slots; `send()`), and he walks on (`release`). Held, the press becomes a breath after `HOLD` (`inhale`): he brightens, a glow grows behind him and motes leave him; let go after `BREATH` more it undoes him all at once (`rush`, `exhale`). From the last state (`dissolve()`) all of him flies into the cloud, which flares (bloom, its particles boosted, the galaxies spin faster; `converge`); then the cloud gives him back whole where he started (`giveBack()`, `bloom`) with the rulito over his head where the card draws it (`RULITO`; `Rulito.vue` in the cyan of the drawing, as a texture in the whole Sid's group), the name lights up and `rulito` sounds (`sceneMilestone('renounced')`). Left alone `IDLE` seconds he takes a state back (`back()`: dots fly from the cloud to him, `bloom`), and another every `BACK`. While whole and untouched, a few motes leave his feet now and then. On a phone upright the frame is bigger than the screen and the view follows him (`pan`, `panFor()`; the farther planes follow less, `PAN`), and while he is the cloud it shows the cloud. The touch target is a DOM element kept over him (`touch-action: none`). Reduced motion keeps the scene still and not played.

## Dana (2) y Sincronicidad (29)

`SceneDana` (id 2) and `SceneSincronicidad` (29) are built from the layers of the Collaborate page (`public/assets/share`, `cocreate`, `inmind`), placed as fractions of a square frame so they keep the illustration's composition. Dana's two friends are separate layers (`public/assets/dana/left.webp`, `right.webp`, cut from the Collaborate picture without the rulito that was drawn over the right one) set further apart than in the drawing. Its verb is Dar y recibir (give and receive), played by sharing the light: the right one (glowing, hand held out) has the little flame, which stays in his hand; a touch on anyone of the island (`GIFTS`: the other friend, the little stone creatures, the mushrooms) sends a spark of it there in an arc (`give()`, `give` sound) and lights him up (`receive()`, `link` sound, each one with his own note; the friend's glow is dots along his outline, `danaAura.json`, the others' a cloud of embers; the giver's own aura, dots along the outline of his picture, `danaGiverAura.json`, shines more with every light he gives). Giving does not make the flame smaller, it grows. When everyone has his light they all send one back at once (`giveBack()`, `converge` sound): a rulito is born over each head, the card's name lights up and `rulito` sounds (`sceneMilestone('light_shared')`); then the lights fade slowly and it can be played again. The idea is in docs/verbos-y-dinamicas.md.

Sincronicidad's verb is Resonar, on time: a glow runs along the four bubbles on a steady beat (`BEAT` 1.3 s; the fire on the fifth beat, a rest on the sixth, `PERIOD`), each brightest and throwing sparks on its beat. A touch within `IN_TIME` (0.28 s) of a bubble's beat makes it resonate (`resonate`, its note of the chord climbing from the egg to the pair) and it stays lit, with a glow behind it at its depth (`L.halos`, bloom); a touch out of time sounds muted (`miss`) and that bubble waits for its next beat (`missed`), so tapping again and again does not catch it. While someone plays the glow sounds softly on every bubble (`pulse`). With the four lit (`synchronised()`) the fire leaps (`flare` of `useFlame.js`), `converge` plays, the gold rulito comes out of the pair's bubble (`Rulito.vue` as a texture at that bubble's depth, floating with it, `L.rulito`), the name lights up and the stage blooms (`sceneMilestone('in_time')`); then the bubbles go out and it can be played again.

How Dana looks: a deep night sky (a gradient on the scene that darkens towards the top and ends in the card colour at the bottom, so it meets the reading), with stars in three planes of particles that shift apart with the pointer (520 faint far ones, the stars of the card, 16 big soft out-of-focus ones close to the eye that keep off the middle); the stars of the card are 640, mostly yellow and red. A small spiral galaxy turns slowly in a corner (top right on a wide screen, top left on a phone upright): `galaxy` (flat dots: a bulge, a faint disc and two logarithmic arms, the inner stars turning faster) over `galaxyGlow` (soft glows on its core and arms). Silent shooting stars (`shooting`, two slots) cross the upper sky every few seconds. A band of dust like a nebula and two blurred islets far behind were tried and taken out. The island is drawn bigger than in the illustration, always `ISLAND` (1.22) times, grown from the line where the friends stand (`PIVOT`; its place in `LAYOUT` is worked out from those two), and `onIsland()` moves with it everything measured on it (`GIFTS` but the friend, their touch boxes, `DRIPS`, `GLINTS`); the friends keep their size. On a phone upright it fills the width and its tips are cut a little. It drips (`drops`: flat, crisp drops with a short trail, from the tips of the moss and the three lowest corners of the crystal), its crystal glints (`glints`) and fireflies wander round it (`fireflies(side)`). On a wide screen the frame is lifted off the bottom a little, so the bigger island has room for its drops.

**Dana in WebGL.** Dana is drawn by the common stage (`useLayerStage.js`, as Paciencia): `layout()` works out the square frame and the rect of every layer in px of the screen from the same percentages the DOM version used (`LAYOUT`, `RULITOS`), and the island, the friends, the two rulitos (their canvases as textures) and the flame hang from one group (`L.isle`) at one depth, which floats; the camera slides 14px with the pointer or the tilt (`SLIDE`, `depthFor`). The particles are groups of `useStardust.js` drawn by the stage in order: the sky (far stars, galaxy, shooting stars, the stars of the card) before every layer, each friend's aura just behind him, half the fireflies behind the right one, the flame's embers behind its ball, and in front of everything what is given (the glows of the little ones, the lights in the air, the sparks), the drops, the glints, the other fireflies and the out-of-focus stars. The groups that follow the island are measured in % of the frame and carried by an affine map of the group taken once a frame (`measure()`, `onCanvas()`), not one projection per particle. The touch targets are DOM elements moved over the island every frame (`placeHits()`, `boxOnScreen`, at least 44px). When the rulitos are whole the stage blooms with the card's name (`stage.bloom`): the rulitos, the flame, its embers, the lights in the air and the sparks.

## Hogar (25)

`SceneHogar` (25) uses its own layers in `public/assets/hogar` (crown, organelles, larva...), each cropped to its box (fractions of the 3240px original, `PIECES` in the component). The inside of the cell is a watery system (`useCellWater.js`): the organelles bob about and the larva floats slowly, bouncing off them and off the cell wall (circles in `PIECES`). The interior and the nucleus are CSS discs; the nucleus's stipple and the orange dust are particles (the halo is a density map of the original, `hogarDust.json`; only a thin fringe stays baked in the crown picture). The crown and its touchable mushroom caps (`hogarCaps.json`) turn together; touching a cap throws spores (`useSparks.js`). The crown moves with the interior (no separate parallax), or a gap shows between the two. Its verb is Arraigar, turned inwards: holding the nucleus (the spark; `.core`, `touch-action: none`, Enter or space for the keyboard) stops time (`calm` in `useCellWater.js` stills the organelles, the crown stops turning) and the larva swims home against the nucleus (`home`); a root of light grows from the foot of every mushroom to the nucleus (`useCellRoots.js`, a 2D canvas drawn again every frame because both ends move, added over the organelles and under the larva), one by one, and each that arrives sends a bead back up and lights its cap (a glow per cap in the crown group) with a `pulse` note climbing the chord; the rulito is born over the larva's head and is whole with the last cap: bloom, `rulito`, a wave out along the roots and spores from every cap. Let go, time runs again and the rulito goes; roots and a glow on the caps stay. The spark's glow is behind the nucleus and is not in the bloom (that pass would draw it without the nucleus over it). The artwork's PNG originals are not kept in the repo (they are in its git history); layers are exported to WebP, never ship PNGs.

## Creatividad (20)

`SceneCreatividad` (20) is built like Hogar, from the layers in `public/assets/creatividad` and `creatividadScene.json` (boxes in % of the 3240px square, circles for collisions, the interior's drawn outline as an SVG path): a crown of corals and weeds that turns slowly, each piece swaying from where it hangs, touchable (squash + spores); inside, the same watery system (`useCellWater.js`) with the organelles bobbing and a jellyfish swimming and bumping into them. The orange halo (`creatividadDust.json`) and the nucleus's fuzzy rim are particles. Its verb is Resonar as free composition (no right answer): the crown is a music box. Touching a coral or a weed (`compose()`) lights it (its picture brighter, a light behind it in the crown group, `L.lights`) and plays its note of the card's chord (`tine` in `synth.js`, a music-box pluck: a coral's note goes by how high it hangs at rest, `height`, across two octaves; a weed's is the bass, rounder and longer); touching a lit one puts it out (`miss`). With anything lit the crown turns faster (`IDLE_TURN` 1.4°/s to `PLAY_TURN` 30°/s, one turn in 12 s, eased; the crown's turn is integrated in `music()`, not taken from the time) and each lit piece that crosses the comb at the top (`COMB`, a soft light over the crown that does not turn, `L.comb`) plays again (`sound()`: the piece flashes, sparks at the comb), so the place of each piece round the crown is its time in the tune and its height its note. The jellyfish swims up under the comb to listen (`home` of `useCellWater.js`, `HOME`) and pulses with every note, with a light behind it (`L.listen`). With seven lit (`TUNE`, `complete()`) `converge` plays, the gold rulito is born over the jellyfish's bell (`Rulito.vue` as a texture that follows it), `rulito` sounds when it is whole, the name lights up and the stage blooms (`sceneMilestone('tune_composed')`); the crown plays one more turn a little faster, then every piece goes out as it passes the comb for the last time (`final`), the rulito's tail catches its head and the crown slows down. While the rulito lives a touch only squashes. An unfinished tune left alone for three turns (`IDLE_TURNS`) ends the same way. With reduced motion the scene is still and is not played (as Sincronicidad).

## Evolución Espiritual (22)

`SceneEvolucion` (22) is built like Creatividad from `public/assets/evolucion` and `evolucionScene.json`: a fern crown with eight glowing bulbs on stalks and four mushrooms (they sway from where they hang and can be touched: their glow flares and sparks fly), all turning slowly with a canvas of glow particles behind them (the stippled rings of `evolucionHalo.json` and a cloud of dots around each bulb and mushroom; `useStardust.js` measures a canvas by its layout size so it can sit inside a turning element); the watery interior has the organelles bobbing and a curled-up being bumping into them. The interior's drawn silhouette is drawn a little bigger than the artwork's, so the turning crown never leaves a gap (Creatividad does the same). Its verb is Encender (rub and sling): the bulbs, the mushrooms and their blue buds start grey (`uSaturate`, `fade()` of their dot groups in `useStardust.js`, the buds' disc colour). Rubbing inside the cell (`cellHit`, a DOM circle kept over it, `touch-action: none` so the phone does not take the rub for a scroll and cancel it; the crown and below still let the page scroll; `move()` adds the finger's path to `heat`, which cools slowly) warms the being (a glow behind it, `L.fire`, embers and sparks from `useSparks.js`, the interior tinted warm with `uTint`, `kindle` sound) until it catches fire (`ignite()`). Then it is a sling: pressing and pulling back draws a band and the way it will go (`aimGroup`, a `dynamic` particle group) and letting go (`launch()`) gives the swimmer of `useCellWater.js` that velocity; while it flies it goes past the organelles (`ghost`) and every knock on the wall (`hitWall()`) lights up to two unlit ones on that side (`catchFire()`: their colour, a glow behind each, `L['lit-…']`, their dots, `link` sound); after `FUEL` knocks, or slowed down, its fire goes out (`goOut()`). The water keeps the being's centre `WALL` from the wall (its picture is taller than its circle), more while the rulito is over its head. With all twelve lit (`crown()`) the crown is a beacon (`L.beacon`, behind the cell), the being uncurls a little, `resolve` plays (B7 to E major), then the gold rulito is born over the being's head (`Rulito.vue` as a texture that follows it), the name lights up and the stage blooms; then everything fades back to grey and it can be played again (`sceneMilestone('crown_lit')`). The idea is in docs/verbos-y-dinamicas.md.

## Khanti (5)

`SceneKhanti` (5) is built from the layers in `public/assets/khanti` (`khantiLayers.json`: box, area and colour of each, in % of the 2160px square): the pink bodies, the creatures in the flasks (each with its own small life, `LIFE` in the component), the glass reflections, the outlines and the corks, all floating as one group. The lavender thread is a route (`khantiLine.json`: samples of x, y and half-thickness, traced from the artwork and rerouted so it never crosses a bubble) along which fourteen brush strokes live along it (every third one long), each between hiding places where the route goes behind the pink (they grow out of one and sink into the next, so they never start or end in the open air), fading at both ends; the mouse makes the nearest ones lean towards it and pressing (or a finger dragging) pulls the nearest one up to it, and a stroke going by a flask lights the creature in it for a moment; they are drawn as ribbons twice, under the pink and over it, the second copy masked to the stretches where the route crosses in front, so they weave over and under the tubes; they are decoration and do not answer the finger. Nine flasks have a cork and their own pink picture (`khantiFlasks.json`, `f1..f9`). Its verb is Fluir (accompany the current): the pink of the body and the flasks starts still (`STILL`: less saturated, less bright, hardly any water light). A current of live pink (`drawFlow()`, a canvas between the pink and the flasks, as a texture) is born in the central sign and goes along one route through the tubes and the nine flasks (`khantiFlow.json`: x, y and the tube's half-width every 8px of the 2160 square, traced from the artwork, with where it enters and leaves each flask), at an uneven pace (`paceAt()`); a finger on its head (`headHit`, a DOM circle kept over it, `touch-action: none`; a finger is followed from further away than the mouse, `TOUCH_REACH`, and a touch that starts near the head does not scroll the page whatever it lands on, a creature or a cork beside it included: `onTouchStart` cancels it, as Vipassana does with Sid; anywhere else the page scrolls), the mouse near it or Enter held on it goes with it: it widens, shines, runs at its pace and sounds (`flow`); alone it thins and crawls, never back. A corked flask stops it (its cork pulses); pressing the cork pops it, the old pink evaporates (sinks behind a sliding mask, vapour rises, the creature goes up and away) and the tubes round it empty a stretch (pale glass drawn over them); once empty, the current fills it with live pink, without its creature, and goes on. With the nine filled the network turns live, the rulito is born over the central sign (`Rulito.vue` as a texture), the name lights up and the stage blooms (`sceneMilestone('current_flowed')`); then the current fades, the corks come back, the creatures come back and everything is still again. The central sign only flashes. The blue dots of the sky and the lavender specks are particles. Touching a creature makes it flash and throw sparks of its colour (`useSparks.js`, from a canvas in front).

## Pañña (3)

`ScenePanna` (id 3): the line work (Sid, branches, leaves, mushroom outlines, the walls of the forest) is a set of layers in `public/assets/panna`, each cropped to its own box (`pannaLayers.json`, fractions of the original 2160px square). All the colour (sun, Sid's aura, fireflies' halos, blue mushrooms, the mushrooms' pink glow) and the stippled edge of the floor are particles behind the line work, drawn from density maps of the original stipple (`pannaClouds.json`); the floor itself is a drawn SVG silhouette. The spiral from the sun to Sid's forehead and the sparks are `dynamic` particle groups in front. The fireflies, the blue mushrooms, Sid and the sun can be touched (flare + sparks; the sun makes everything answer). The particles stay hidden (`manual` + `reveal()`) until all the drawings are decoded. Its verb is Ver: the nine lights (sun, Sid's third eye, the big pink mushroom in the middle, four fireflies, two blue mushrooms) start unjoined and are joined by dragging from one to another with threads of dots (`helix()`, one `dynamic` group with a slot per thread, `join()`): each starts as a point, swells into a double helix and closes into a point, its dots taking the colours of the two lights it joins, with a soft glow under it (the `glow` group), and joining two plays one note of each light in the card's chord (`link` in `synth.js`, each light has a height in `PITCH`). There is one figure to see per try, drawn at random from `FIGURES` (cross, diamond, hexagon, two triangles: 4 to 6 threads, not every light in every figure) and kept until it is seen; the threads of the figure are firm and loud and the others dim and soft, if nothing is touched for ~9 s the figure draws itself faint and goes (`ghost`), the aura swells up to 14% as the figure's threads are joined (`keepLit`, `swellAura`, `grow()` in `useStardust.js`), and when the threads are as many as the figure's they are judged: if it is the figure (`seen()`) the threads carry their light to the third eye (`converge` sound), the eye opens (`openEye()`, `open` sound), the rulito is born over Sid's head and the card's name lights up, and the threads go out with the rulito so another figure comes; if it is not (`unravel()`, `unravel` sound) they go out softly and it starts again with the same figure (`sceneMilestone('pattern_seen')` and `'pattern_reset'`; the idea is in docs/verbos-y-dinamicas.md). The eye, the sun and the pink mushroom are separate hit areas (`touch-action: none`); Sid's body still lets the page scroll. The centre lives in a square frame; the surroundings are a slightly bigger square whose left and right halves are anchored to the screen's sides, with a vertically flipped copy above it so the forest reaches the top.
