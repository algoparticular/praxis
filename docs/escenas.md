# Escenas de las cartas

Detalle de cómo está construida cada escena inmersiva. Estaba en `CLAUDE.md` y se movió acá, sin cambios y en inglés, para que `CLAUDE.md` quede corto. Qué hace cada escena al tocarla: `registro-cartas-vivas.md`. Qué propone cada verbo: `verbos-y-dinamicas.md`.

## Paciencia (27) y Vipassana (31)

`ScenePaciencia` (id 27) and `SceneVipassana` (id 31) are built from separate layers that move with depth (pointer / tilt / scroll parallax via `useParallax.js`, plus each layer's own idle motion). Vipassana reuses the landing hero: `useCellField`, `useHeroSpiral` and `useCharacterLife`. Vipassana's verb is Soltar (let go) and the scene has a goal: the field holds 21 cells (15 on low-power devices), each one something to let go of. A press on a cell lets go of it: it bursts into dots that stay gone (`cellField.popCell()`, `release` sound through `onEvent`). The dotted spiral at his third eye is the count and it gets shorter with every cell that goes (`spiral.progress()`); if nothing is touched for 7 s one of the cells that are left glows now and then (`cellField.hint()`, no words). A press on the water is one breath of the whole scene (`breathCurve.js`): `cellField.ripple()` sends a ring through the water (a shader plane) that shoves the cells (its strength follows how long the press was held); when the ring reaches the character `life.inhale()` and `cellField.breathe()` draw everything in towards him (`breath` sound) and as he lets it out his spiral lights up (`onExhale`). It is the rhythm of the scene and is not needed to finish it. Holding the character himself is holding his breath in, which is clinging (`inhale` sound): his spiral shortens (`spiral.hold()`, 3.2s) until it is a point at his third eye, the field leans in and the cells nearest to him are caught one after the other, a third of them (`CATCH` in `useCellField.js`: 7 of 21), so three held breaths are enough to let go of all of them; held a moment longer, the caught cells and the matter orbit him like electrons round a nucleus, a cosmic mandala (`orbitW` in `useCellField.js`): six orbits crossing at him, 30 degrees apart (three small quick ones between three wide slow ones, turning in alternate directions), with the orbits staying put and Sid rising as they form (`RISE` in `breathCurve.js`: 17% of his own height up and 7% of his width to the left (`SIDE`: 4% by measure plus what the lean towards the finger and the scene's depth parallax take), so that the mass of his body, which is not at the middle of his picture, ends in the middle of the orbits; `getRise` in `useCharacterLife.js`) and going back to where he was on release, the enormous cells on an orbit shrinking while it lasts (`ORBIT_CELL_MAX`; the small ones keep their size) so that he is what matters, and with the electrons evenly spaced along each and coming forward (bigger, brighter) and going back (smaller, dimmer) as they go round; the orbits themselves are never drawn, they are only in how things move; and on release (`exhale` sound) the spiral comes back out shining, the big ring runs from him (`ripple(x, y, 1, impact)` throws cells and matter much harder) and `cellField.letGoCaught()` lets go of every cell he had caught as the ring reaches it. Easter egg, not announced: held far longer (`POWER_AT` in `useCellField.js`, 4.2s at the top of the orbit) every cell left joins the orbits (`powered()`, `power` event: `star` sound), and on release the ring is harder and faster and they all break together (`sceneMilestone('power_breath')`). Whether the finger is on Sid is read from a precomputed mask (`characterMask.js`, made by `scripts/make-character-mask.mjs`; reading a canvas at runtime fails in desktop Safari), and the scene stops the browser's own press behaviours on him (selection, drag, Force Touch lookup). When no cell is left (`startWin` in the scene) the dotted spiral goes (`spiral.fade()`) and the rulito (`Rulito.vue`, in the pink of the spiral, small and floating clear of his head) is drawn, the card's name lights up and the `rulito` sounds; then `cellField.gather()` brings every cell back (`bloom` sounds) as the rulito's tail catches up with its head, and the spiral comes back (`sceneMilestone('all_released')`; the idea is in docs/verbos-y-dinamicas.md). Reduced motion keeps the scene static: it cannot be played yet. Paciencia is the first card with a verb as a game (Arraigar: the pine cone is held, a network of golden roots (`useRoots.js`, a canvas over the mushrooms that stays while the rulito lives and fades away a moment after it has gone, `ROOTS_LINGER` and `ROOTS_FADE`; the next hold grows another network) spreads under the field, its charge fills, and a rulito over the central Sama's head, drawn in code on a canvas by `Rulito.vue`, is born as a dot, stretches into a line and curls into its loop, and when let go its tail catches up with its head until it is gone; see `docs/verbos-y-dinamicas.md`). Its boat is in pieces (`public/assets/paciencia/boat.webp`, an atlas, and `pacienciaScene.json`, made by `scripts/convert-paciencia.mjs` from the layers in `originales/paciencia`, in the git history from commit c59acb9), so its crew lives, each in its own way, with their antennae (cut from each outline at the top of the head) swaying in the wind (`wind`): the one at the left looks around and blinks, the one in the middle rows (the oar turns in his grip and he leans into it; the rulito rides on his head), the little one hops now and then, the one on the spiral rocks to and fro humming and the sleepy one at the right end nods off and wakes with a start. Its ground is a strip of its own (`paciencia/grass.webp` and `mushrooms.webp`, from the layers in `originales/paciencia/paciencia-piso`, also from c59acb9), half as wide as the old one for its height, so it is laid three times side by side, the outer two mirrored (no seam); the roots and the spores work on the middle two strips' width, as before. The mushrooms and the little balls of the coral lean and breathe on their stems (`sway` in `useLayerStage.js`, about the caps and balls the converter found in `pacienciaScene.json`; the picture is shown in a taller box so the caps at its top have room), and when one is about to let go of its spores (`onPuff` in `useSpores.js`) it gathers itself, trembling, and pops as they come out; every 5 to 9 s one sneezes a bigger puff on its own. The pine cone is `paciencia/cone.webp` (layers 1 and 2), on the cut stem in the gap in the middle of the strip; `collaborate/boat.webp` keeps its rulito for the Collaborate page. Paciencia's sun is not a picture: a disc in code (`SUN`, measured in fractions of its old box) with its stippled rim as live particles made in the scene (`sunRim`, a `dynamic` group that follows the disc as it breathes); `collaborate/sun.webp` stays for the Collaborate page. Vipassana's breath is taller than it is wide: part of the swell `useCharacterLife` gives goes into `sy` and a little comes off `sx`.

**The WebGL stage (every scene).** Since the pilot in Paciencia, the scenes are drawn whole in WebGL by `useLayerStage.js`: one canvas and one renderer per scene, every picture a plane at its depth, and the camera slides with the pointer or the tilt of the phone (`stage.look`) with its lens shifted so the focal plane stays put, which is the parallax: each part moves as far as it did in the DOM version (`amp / (SLIDE + amp)` gives its depth). Everything is laid out in px of the screen at rest, computed from the CSS of the DOM version (`layout()` or `place()` in each scene). The particles of `useStardust.js` are drawn among the layers (`{ stage, groups: [{ order }] }`), and can follow a layer (`shift` with `stage.drift(z)`) or turn with it (`turn`). Touch targets are DOM elements moved over what they belong to every frame (`boxOnScreen`, `toScreen`), so they still take a finger and the keyboard. Shader effects: `wind` (Paciencia's grass, Evolución's fronds, Pañña's leaves), `shimmer` (the air over Paciencia's sun, the organelles seen through the water, Sincronicidad's bubbles, Vipassana's rays), `water` (the light of the water across the inside of Hogar, Creatividad and Evolución, and Khanti's pink: a net of thin bright lines, `CAUSTIC`; broad soft bands were tried first and blurred the parts of the cells), `sheen` (a band of light over Khanti's glass), `sink` (Khanti's flasks empty in the shader), `blur`, `vignette`, `clip` and mirrors (`sx`/`sy`, Pañña's forest), discs and glows drawn in code. Canvases drawn by a scene (the rulito, Paciencia's roots, Creatividad's and Evolución's interiors, Khanti's thread, Pañña's floor) are shown as textures; when such a canvas changes size (a resize, a phone turned) `refresh()` makes its texture again, since a texture keeps the size it was first uploaded with. A scene builds its layers and registers `place()` before it starts the particle groups measured on them, so on a resize they are made again after the layout. The bloom pass draws into a target a quarter of the screen's size: the particle groups shrink their points to match while it runs (`onBloomScale`; drawn at full size there they came out four times too big, and on a real GPU rows of dots melted into a white smear), and the blurred light is added as light only, leaving the canvas's alpha as it was (adding alpha too darkened what was behind a see-through canvas, a scene's CSS sky: a black box round the rulito). Bloom only while `stage.bloom` is above zero: the rulito moments of Paciencia, Hogar, Dana, Pañña, Vipassana, Evolución, Khanti and Sincronicidad (and, softer, while its bubbles are lit), with the same value as `--name-glow`. Vipassana draws its field of cells with its own three.js scene and camera in the stage's renderer (`useVipassanaField.js` through `stage.underlay()`, a copy of the landing's `useCellField.js`, which stays as it was); Sid is drawn on the focal plane so his hidden element (moved by `useCharacterLife`, which hands the stage each frame with `onFrame`) is exactly where he is seen, and his glow is his own picture blurred and tinted in the shader instead of a CSS drop-shadow. Without WebGL a card shows its flat illustration (`stage()` in `cardScenes.js`). The tilt on iOS needs a permission that is asked once in the life of the app, on the first touch of a visit (`useTilt.js`, remembered in `localStorage`); without it the parallax follows the finger.

**Paciencia in WebGL (the pilot).** Paciencia is drawn whole in WebGL by `useLayerStage.js`: one canvas and one renderer for the scene, every picture a plane at its depth, and the camera slides with the pointer or the tilt of the phone (`stage.look`) with its lens shifted so the focal plane stays put, which is the parallax (the sun moves 8px at the edge of a swipe, the boat 22, the ground 40; `depthFor` in the scene). The layout is the one of the DOM version, computed in `layout()` in px of the screen; on a phone the strip of coral and mushrooms is 30% bigger, so the pine cone sits higher. Shader effects: the grass sways in the wind (`wind`), the air trembles over the sun (`shimmer`), which is also a little out of focus (`blur`) and has a soft light that breathes (`glow`); the pine cone is lit by its material (`bright`, `saturate`) and a halo, not by CSS filters. The spores (`useSpores.js` with `points: true`) are particle groups of `useStardust.js` drawn by the stage between the boat and the grass, and the gold sparks in front; the roots and the rulito are their 2D canvases shown as textures. The touch target of the pine cone is a DOM element moved over it every frame (`boxOnScreen`), so it still takes a finger and the keyboard; it has `touch-action: none`, so a finger that drifts a little while holding is not taken for a scroll that lets go of the hold. When the rulito is completed the stage blooms (`stage.bloom`, the same value as `--name-glow`): the rulito, the cone, its halo, the roots and the sparks are drawn again small, blurred and added on top; it only runs while it is above zero. Without WebGL the card shows its flat illustration (`stage()` in `cardScenes.js`). The tilt on iOS needs a permission that is asked once in the life of the app, on the first touch of a visit (`useTilt.js`, remembered in `localStorage`); without it the parallax follows the finger.
## Nekkhamma (0)

`SceneNekkhamma` (0) is built from the layers in `public/assets/nekkhamma` and `nekkhammaScene.json`, both made by `scripts/convert-nekkhamma.mjs` from the exported PNGs (each picture cropped to its box, in px of the 2160 square; the glowing buds each cut out on its own, `buds`, with a soft round mask; every dot of the layers that are fields of dots, as [x, y, radius, colour]: the stars, the pink specks and the cosmic cloud; and points of the whole Sid: `body`, every 11 px inside him with its colour, and `outline`, along his dark line). The sky is a CSS gradient in the card's colour; the wind strokes, the planet (its ring a separate picture; moved to the left, `PLANET`, out of the way of the cloud's galaxy), the ground (stretched to both sides of the screen) with its plants (swaying in the shader, `WIND`), the buds, the mark, and Sid are planes at their depths. Each bud breathes at its own pace (scale, brightness and a glow behind it) and sways on its stem with the same wave as the shader's wind, worked out for where it stands on the plants. The illustration draws four Sids, each more undone than the last; the scene uses only the whole one, moved to the middle (`DX`) and a little down so his feet stand in the ground (`DY`), and undoes him itself: his picture has the `dissolve` effect of `useLayerStage.js` (`uDissolve.y` 0..1: gone from the bottom up along a ragged edge of value noise a few px wide, which glows in the given colour as it eats in). He is tied by threads of dots (`threads`, a `dynamic` particle group, `threadGroup`; each a sagging, swaying curve from a point of him to one of `ANCHORS`, its dots flowing slowly towards him) to each of the seven glowing buds on the ground, his possessions. Its verb is Soltar: a touch on a bud (a DOM circle kept over it, `placeTies()`) or on him cuts a thread (`cut()`): its dots burst into motes that rise to the cloud (`release`), and he comes undone as far as the threads cut (`level` eases towards it); where each thread holds him climbs as he comes undone. As the edge passes the dots of his line (`outline`) they show where he was, as the illustration draws him undone (`lineGroup`). The cloud only exists as he lets go: the illustration's cloud, moved over him (`CLOUD`), spread sideways with two twins of each dot on his other side and thinned towards its sides and bottom, is shown in `ORDER` (from over his head outwards) as far as he is gone (`follow()`), and every other dot shown is a mote that rises to it from the edge eating him (`edgePoint()`, `flyers`, a `dynamic` group of slots; `send()`); its glow and a little spiral galaxy of particles (`useGalaxy.js`, the same as Dana's, in the colours of the cloud) come with it. The threads left are the count; left alone `HINT` seconds (7), one of them glows in a wave towards him, with its bud: that is one to cut. Held, the press becomes a breath after `HOLD` (`inhale`): he brightens, a glow grows behind him and motes leave him; let go after `BREATH` more every thread is cut, one quickly after the other (`exhale`, faster `rate`). With none left and nothing of his body, his line's dots rise into the cloud after `LINGER` (or at once with another touch) and he is the cloud (`dissolve()`, `converge`): it flares, then comes down, each dot back to his edge, as he is given back whole from the feet up (`bloom`), the rulito is born over his head where the card draws it (`RULITO`, `Rulito.vue` in the cyan of the drawing), the name lights up and `rulito` sounds (`sceneMilestone('renounced')`); when it goes his threads grow back slowly. Left alone `IDLE` seconds a thread grows back and he takes back a part (`back()`, `bloom`), and the cloud thins with it, another every `BACK`; whole and untouched, a few motes leave his feet now and then. Holding him does not bloom (the light behind him is enough); his threads are not in the bloom. Reduced motion keeps the scene still and not played. The planet's body is not a picture: a pink disc with its dark outline in code (`PLANET_AT`, `PLANET_PINK`, `PLANET_EDGE`); only its ring is. Sid breathes from his feet (`sy`, `sx`), and the dots of his line breathe with him.

## Dana (2) y Sincronicidad (29)

`SceneDana` (id 2) and `SceneSincronicidad` (29) are built from the layers of the Collaborate page (`public/assets/share`, `cocreate`, `inmind`), placed as fractions of a square frame so they keep the illustration's composition. Dana's two friends are separate layers (`public/assets/dana/left.webp`, `right.webp`, cut from the Collaborate picture without the rulito that was drawn over the right one) set further apart than in the drawing. Its verb is Dar y recibir (give and receive), played by sharing the light: the right one (glowing, hand held out) has the little flame, which stays in his hand; a touch on anyone of the island (`GIFTS`: the other friend, the little stone creatures, the mushrooms) sends a spark of it there in an arc (`give()`, `give` sound) and lights him up (`receive()`, `link` sound, each one with his own note; the friend's glow is dots along his outline, `danaAura.json`, the others' a cloud of embers; the giver's own aura, dots along the outline of his picture, `danaGiverAura.json`, shines more with every light he gives). Giving does not make the flame smaller, it grows. When everyone has his light they all send one back at once (`giveBack()`, `converge` sound): a rulito is born over each head, the card's name lights up and `rulito` sounds (`sceneMilestone('light_shared')`); then the lights fade slowly and it can be played again. The idea is in docs/verbos-y-dinamicas.md. Dana's two friends breathe from their feet, each at its own pace, and sway a little; every `GLANCE` s (8) the one on the left turns to the one with the flame and he answers with a small nod (only a little: the flame is in his hand).

Sincronicidad's verb is Resonar, on time: a glow runs along the four bubbles on a steady beat (`BEAT` 1.3 s; the fire on the fifth beat, a rest on the sixth, `PERIOD`), each brightest and throwing sparks on its beat. A touch within `IN_TIME` (0.28 s) of a bubble's beat makes it resonate (`resonate`, its note of the chord climbing from the egg to the pair) and it stays lit, with a glow behind it at its depth (`L.halos`, bloom); a touch out of time sounds muted (`miss`) and that bubble waits for its next beat (`missed`), so tapping again and again does not catch it. While someone plays the glow sounds softly on every bubble (`pulse`). With the four lit (`synchronised()`) the fire leaps (`flare` of `useFlame.js`) and is heard (`ignite`), `converge` plays, the gold rulito comes out of the pair's bubble (`Rulito.vue` as a texture at that bubble's depth, floating with it, `L.rulito`), the name lights up and the stage blooms (`sceneMilestone('in_time')`); then the bubbles go out and it can be played again.

How Dana looks: a deep night sky (a gradient on the scene that darkens towards the top and ends in the card colour at the bottom, so it meets the reading), with stars in three planes of particles that shift apart with the pointer (520 faint far ones, the stars of the card, 16 big soft out-of-focus ones close to the eye that keep off the middle); the stars of the card are 640, mostly yellow and red. A small spiral galaxy turns slowly in a corner (top right on a wide screen, top left on a phone upright): `useGalaxy.js` (flat dots: a bulge, a faint disc and two logarithmic arms, the inner stars turning faster, over soft glows on its core and arms), shared with Nekkhamma's cloud. Silent shooting stars (`shooting`, two slots) cross the upper sky every few seconds. A band of dust like a nebula and two blurred islets far behind were tried and taken out. The island is drawn bigger than in the illustration, always `ISLAND` (1.22) times, grown from the line where the friends stand (`PIVOT`; its place in `LAYOUT` is worked out from those two), and `onIsland()` moves with it everything measured on it (`GIFTS` but the friend, their touch boxes, `DRIPS`, `GLINTS`); the friends keep their size. On a phone upright it fills the width and its tips are cut a little. It drips (`drops`: flat, crisp drops with a short trail, from the tips of the moss and the three lowest corners of the crystal), its crystal glints (`glints`) and fireflies wander round it (`fireflies(side)`). On a wide screen the frame is lifted off the bottom a little, so the bigger island has room for its drops.

**Dana in WebGL.** Dana is drawn by the common stage (`useLayerStage.js`, as Paciencia): `layout()` works out the square frame and the rect of every layer in px of the screen from the same percentages the DOM version used (`LAYOUT`, `RULITOS`), and the island, the friends, the two rulitos (their canvases as textures) and the flame hang from one group (`L.isle`) at one depth, which floats; the camera slides 14px with the pointer or the tilt (`SLIDE`, `depthFor`). The particles are groups of `useStardust.js` drawn by the stage in order: the sky (far stars, galaxy, shooting stars, the stars of the card) before every layer, each friend's aura just behind him, half the fireflies behind the right one, the flame's embers behind its ball, and in front of everything what is given (the glows of the little ones, the lights in the air, the sparks), the drops, the glints, the other fireflies and the out-of-focus stars. The groups that follow the island are measured in % of the frame and carried by an affine map of the group taken once a frame (`measure()`, `onCanvas()`), not one projection per particle. The touch targets are DOM elements moved over the island every frame (`placeHits()`, `boxOnScreen`, at least 44px). When the rulitos are whole the stage blooms with the card's name (`stage.bloom`): the rulitos, the flame, its embers, the lights in the air and the sparks.

## Sacca (6)

`SceneSacca` (6) is built from the layers in `public/assets/sacca` and `saccaScene.json`, both made by `scripts/convert-sacca.mjs` from the exported PNGs (they came in a folder named `originales/sila` by mistake; they are in the git history). The sky is a CSS gradient worked out from the card (periwinkle above, lavender and peach as it goes down to the dunes; its stops follow the frame, `place()`). The moon is not a picture: a disc drawn in code (`MOON`, measured on the card's solid middle) with its ragged rim as live particles made in the scene (`MOON_RIM`), with the stipple round it. The boat (hull and line), Sid's line work, the mast, the lookout (with his arm) and the dunes are pictures; the frame is a square resting on the bottom of the screen, on a phone upright as big as the boat's scrolls allow across (`BOAT_SPAN`), and the dunes reach both sides with a mirrored copy on each side. The stipple round the moon is particles (its dots, from the illustration). Sid's body and its glow are not pictures: the converter traces the inside of his outline into a polygon (`sid`), which the scene fills on a canvas shown as a texture under his lines (`drawSid()`), with a soft light behind it and a stipple of particles made in code round the shape, denser near him and longer towards his legs (`SID_DOTS`); both breathe and brighten with his heart (Fa). Every cloud is cut out on its own and packed in one atlas (`clouds.webp`; a picture of the stage can show a part of a texture, `crop`, and pictures with the same src share one texture, `useLayerStage.js`), so the clouds are not held by the frame: they are spread over the whole sky (`place()`; a little bigger on a phone, where they are touched), at the depth of their colour (`DEPTH.clouds`), drifting to the left at their own pace and wrapping round (`span`). The boat and what it carries rock as one group (`L.boat`); Sid's stipple turns and shifts with it (`turn`, `shift`). Its verb is Resonar as an echo: the lookout sings a phrase of the four colours (`phrase`, `singNow()`, `singNote()`, `sing` in `synth.js`, one note every `GAP`), each note lighting every cloud of its colour (`shine`, a glow behind each cloud, `L.cloudGlows`) while he stretches up in his cup with a light of that colour behind him (`L.voice`); it is repeated by touching clouds of those colours in the same order (`touchCloud()`: `resonate`, sparks and its light flying to him; a wrong one is `miss`, the lights go out and he sings it again, slower). Each colour is the note of its chakra (`COLOURS`): light blue Sol, salmon Re, blue-green Fa (Sid's heart), pale pink Si. Every cloud has a touch box (at least 44px; none behind the moon), and since the boxes overlap a touch goes to the cloud whose middle is nearest for its size among the boxes it is on (`pick()`). How to play is shown without words: each note he sings flies from him to every cloud of its colour in sight (three flyers, a head and a tail, `FLIGHT`) and lights them as it arrives (`arrive`), so he is seen to be the one singing and the clouds what answers; on the person's turn, left alone `HINT` seconds (3.5), the clouds of the colour that comes next breathe (`next`, `hinted`: a hint, not the answer), and four beads in the air under the boat, between its hull and the dunes (a `dynamic` group, `beadGroup`, `BEADS_AT`: a navy ring round a cream dot) take the colour of each note repeated right and go blank again on a mistake. Left alone `AGAIN` seconds (7) he sings it again; a touch on him asks for it. Between songs he keeps watch from the cup, looking to one side and the other (`WATCH`, `gaze`), and `PREP` (0.8 s) before he sings he turns to the front and draws himself up, so a song is seen coming. Sid breathes in the boat (his fill and his lines scale together about the bottom of his shape, `sidPivot()`), and fine grains of sand blow off the dunes to the left, in gusts (`sandGroup`, a `dynamic` group). Repeated whole (`complete()`), every cloud turns the cream of the moon (`one`, `uTint`), their light flies to the lookout, the card's G7 resolves to C major (`resolve`, as in Evolución and Creatividad: the root), the rulito is born over him where the card draws it (`Rulito.vue` as a texture in the boat group, in the peach of the drawn one), the name lights up and `rulito` sounds (`sceneMilestone('echo_sung')`); then the clouds get their colours back and he sings a new phrase. The moon is not in the bloom (so big and light, it washed out the lookout and the rulito). Reduced motion keeps the scene still and not played.

## Addhitthana (7)

`SceneAddhitthana` (7) is built from the layers in `public/assets/addhitthana` and `addhitthanaScene.json`, both made by `scripts/convert-addhitthana.mjs` from the exported PNGs (`originales/aditana`, kept in the repo until the full moon arrives; layer 24 came empty). Particular rows a boat through a storm at night under a moon the dark is eating. Every cloud is a picture of its own (`c<layer>`, in the order the card stacks them, `CLOUDS` in the converter), in three planes (`PLANE`: the high ones round the moon, the ones in the middle behind the boat and the low ones in front); the ones that touch a side or the top of the card have a mirrored copy past it (`sides` in the json, `mirrorOf()`), so a wide screen and the sky over the frame on a phone are not cut. On a phone the sky (the moon and the high clouds) is lifted towards the top of the screen (`lift`). The shadows of Particular and the hull (layers 29 and 34) are baked into their pictures by the converter, black at 17 and 26%, as the card darkens them. The stipples (the dark that eats the moon, the loose dots of the aura and of the little light) are particles; where the dots run into each other they stay in the picture (`CLUMP`). The rulito the card draws (39) is only a place; it will be drawn in code. The storm is not a layer: `rain()` in `useLayerStage.js` draws it in a shader, three times: the wavy lines of the wind on the sky behind everything (`uHatch`, level lines of a noise carried by the wind), rain behind the boat and sparser, bigger rain in front of everything; the scene adds up how far the rain has fallen and the wind has carried its lines (`uFall`, `uFlow`), so a gust changes their speed without a jump. The wind (`windAt()`) blows to the left, a slow swell with gusts: it slants the rain, carries the lines, pushes the clouds (the near ones more) and the boat, which it carries off the middle (never past the edges of the screen, so it can always be rescued: `reach`) and a spring brings back (`boat`), rocking and leaning with its speed; the fern in the boat sways with it (`wind`). Now and then a lightning flickers (`uFlash`, the clouds and the moon lit). The little light by the boat is alive: it hovers in its place, the wind does not move it, flickering and breathing. `calm` (0 the storm .. 1 over) puts the storm out: the rain stops, the wind drops, the high clouds open round the moon, the low ones sink and the dark that eats the moon goes. Its verb is Arraigar: the little light is the middle, the one thing the wind does not move. Holding Particular (`hold()`, a DOM target over his aura with `touch-action: none`, or Enter held) he rows (the oar strokes, `row`): the boat's spring pulls it back under the light, stiffer, and the storm hardly rocks it. `steady` is how far it is under the light and still (`NEAR`, `STILL`); while it is past a half, `grown` fills in `HOLD` seconds (4.2) and the rulito is drawn over his head (`Rulito.vue` as a texture in the boat group, in the pink of the drawn one, where the card draws it), with a `kindle` on each quarter, and the storm softens.
