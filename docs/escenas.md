# Escenas de las cartas

Detalle de cómo está construida cada escena inmersiva. Estaba en `CLAUDE.md` y se movió acá, sin cambios y en inglés, para que `CLAUDE.md` quede corto. Qué hace cada escena al tocarla: `registro-cartas-vivas.md`. Qué propone cada verbo: `verbos-y-dinamicas.md`.

## The ghost hand (`useGhostHand.js`)

The hint every game gives when it is left alone: a fingertip of light, made of dots (two `useStardust` groups drawn by the scene's own stage, over everything), that comes in, does the gesture on the real place and goes. It shows *how* to play (hold, rub, sling, drag, slide, tap in time, repeat in order), which a glow alone cannot. No words. Used today by the first card of each verb: `ScenePaciencia.vue`, `SceneVipassana.vue`, `SceneKhanti.vue`, `SceneViriya.vue`, `SceneEvolucion.vue`, `SceneDana.vue`, `SceneSincronicidad.vue` and `ScenePanna.vue` (the models), and since then `SceneHogar.vue` and `SceneAddhitthana.vue` (hold, on the nucleus and on Particular), `SceneNekkhamma.vue`, `SceneUpekkha.vue`, `SceneCiclos.vue` and `ScenePerdon.vue` (tap on what to let go of, every third time hold Sid; navy in Upekkha and Ciclos), and `SceneCreatividad.vue`, `SceneSila.vue` and `SceneSacca.vue` (tap on a piece not lit yet; three taps in time on the heart; the phrase's clouds in order).

```js
import { createGhostHand, gesture, HINT_AFTER } from '../../composables/useGhostHand.js';
hand = createGhostHand(stage);              // after the stage is made; hand?.destroy() on unmount
// in the loop, when the game wants showing:
if (hand?.due(HINT_AFTER, !held && !won)) hand.play(gesture.hold(someHit.value, 2.4));
```

- **Points** are `[x, y]` in the canvas's CSS px, a function returning one (so it follows what moves) or a DOM element (its centre: the scenes' `hit` elements already sit over what is touched).
- **Gestures** (`gesture`): `tap(P)` (`TAP_LEAD`, 0.85 s, is how long it takes to press, so a tap can be started early to land on a beat), `taps([P…], gap)` in order, `hold(P, s)` (a ring of dots fills round the tip), `rub(P, span)`, `sling(P, toward, pull)` (presses, pulls back away from `toward`, lets go with a burst towards it), `drag(A, B)`, `slide(path, dur, { even })` with `path(u)` for u from 0 to 1 (`even: true` keeps u to the clock, for a path that carries its own pace: Fluir's `flow.hintPath(len, map, t0)` goes at the current's uneven pace; `SLIDE_LEAD` and `SLIDE_AGAIN` say when a slide, and one after it, start sliding). A script can also be written by hand from its steps: `go`, `path`, `down`, `up`, `ring`, `wait`.
- **When:** always after **7 s** left alone (`HINT_AFTER`). The hand keeps its own count, by the real clock: `due(after, wanted)` is true once nothing has been touched for `after` seconds and it is not already out. `wanted` says whether the game wants showing now (not while it is played, nor while a rulito lives); while it is false the count starts again, so after a win it also waits the whole 7 s. A scene with a hint timer of its own resets it the same way (Pañña). `wait()` counts as a touch, for what the scene takes by keyboard.
- `play(script, { loops })` does the gesture `loops` times (2 by default; a new one replaces the one playing), `stop()` sends it away, `playing` says whether it is out. Any touch or key sends it away at once. With reduced motion it never shows.
- `createGhostHand(stage, { color, shade, order })`: the cream of the brand by default, with a faint navy halo under the tip (0.13) so it reads on light skies. Where the cream does not read (a light scene, or a gesture over something cream) it is navy with a cream halo: `createGhostHand(stage, { color: 0x143246, shade: 0xf7f8f1 })` (Paciencia, Khanti, Viriya); each scene that gets the hand checks its contrast.
- **One chat at a time edits this file**; the scenes only use it.

## Paciencia (27) y Vipassana (31)

`ScenePaciencia` (id 27) and `SceneVipassana` (id 31) are built from separate layers that move with depth (pointer / tilt / scroll parallax via `useParallax.js`, plus each layer's own idle motion). Vipassana reuses the landing hero: `useCellField`, `useHeroSpiral` and `useCharacterLife`. Vipassana's verb is Soltar (let go) and the scene has a goal: the field holds 21 cells (15 on low-power devices), each one something to let go of. A press on a cell lets go of it: it bursts into dots that stay gone (`cellField.popCell()`, `release` sound through `onEvent`) until the win, or until the scene is left alone 15 s (`IDLE_BACK`): then one comes back every 4 s (`cellField.gatherOne()`, `BACK`) and the spiral lengthens again. The dotted spiral at his third eye is the count and it gets shorter with every cell that goes (`spiral.progress()`); if nothing is touched for 7 s one of the cells that are left glows now and then (`cellField.hint()`, no words); left alone 7 s (`HINT_AFTER`) the ghost hand (`useGhostHand.js`) touches that same glowing cell, following it as it drifts (`cellField.hintPoint()`), and every third time holds Sid instead, to show that holding him is a breath (it waits again while he is held or the rulito lives, and so does the glow). A press on the water is one breath of the whole scene (`breathCurve.js`): `cellField.ripple()` sends a ring through the water (a shader plane) that shoves the cells (its strength follows how long the press was held); when the ring reaches the character `life.inhale()` and `cellField.breathe()` draw everything in towards him (`breath` sound) and as he lets it out his spiral lights up (`onExhale`). It is the rhythm of the scene and is not needed to finish it. Holding the character himself is holding his breath in, which is clinging (`inhale` sound): his spiral shortens (`spiral.hold()`, 3.2s) until it is a point at his third eye, the field leans in and the cells nearest to him are caught one after the other, a third of them (`CATCH` in `useCellField.js`: 7 of 21), so three held breaths are enough to let go of all of them; held a moment longer, the caught cells and the matter orbit him like electrons round a nucleus, a cosmic mandala (`orbitW` in `useCellField.js`): six orbits crossing at him, 30 degrees apart (three small quick ones between three wide slow ones, turning in alternate directions), with the orbits staying put and Sid rising as they form (`RISE` in `breathCurve.js`: 17% of his own height up and 7% of his width to the left (`SIDE`: 4% by measure plus what the lean towards the finger and the scene's depth parallax take), so that the mass of his body, which is not at the middle of his picture, ends in the middle of the orbits; `getRise` in `useCharacterLife.js`) and going back to where he was on release, the enormous cells on an orbit shrinking while it lasts (`ORBIT_CELL_MAX`; the small ones keep their size) so that he is what matters, and with the electrons evenly spaced along each and coming forward (bigger, brighter) and going back (smaller, dimmer) as they go round; the orbits themselves are never drawn, they are only in how things move; and on release (`exhale` sound) the spiral comes back out shining, the big ring runs from him (`ripple(x, y, 1, impact)` throws cells and matter much harder) and `cellField.letGoCaught()` lets go of every cell he had caught as the ring reaches it. Easter egg, not announced: held far longer (`POWER_AT` in `useCellField.js`, 4.2s at the top of the orbit) every cell left joins the orbits (`powered()`, `power` event: `star` sound), and on release the ring is harder and faster and they all break together (`sceneMilestone('power_breath')`). Whether the finger is on Sid is read from a precomputed mask (`characterMask.js`, made by `scripts/make-character-mask.mjs`; reading a canvas at runtime fails in desktop Safari), and the scene stops the browser's own press behaviours on him (selection, drag, Force Touch lookup). When no cell is left (`startWin` in the scene) the dotted spiral goes (`spiral.fade()`) and the rulito (`Rulito.vue`, in the pink of the spiral, small and floating clear of his head) is drawn, the card's name lights up and the `rulito` sounds; then `cellField.gather()` brings every cell back (`bloom` sounds) as the rulito's tail catches up with its head, and the spiral comes back (`sceneMilestone('all_released')`; the idea is in docs/verbos-y-dinamicas.md). Reduced motion keeps the scene static: it cannot be played yet. Paciencia is the first card with a verb as a game (Arraigar: the pine cone is held, a network of golden roots (`useRoots.js`, a canvas over the mushrooms that stays while the rulito lives and fades away a moment after it has gone, `ROOTS_LINGER` and `ROOTS_FADE`; the next hold grows another network) spreads under the field, its charge fills, and a rulito over the central Sama's head, drawn in code on a canvas by `Rulito.vue`, is born as a dot, stretches into a line and curls into its loop, and when let go its tail catches up with its head until it is gone; see `docs/verbos-y-dinamicas.md`). Its boat is in pieces (`public/assets/paciencia/boat.webp`, an atlas, and `pacienciaScene.json`, made by `scripts/convert-paciencia.mjs` from the layers in `originales/paciencia`, in the git history from commit c59acb9), so its crew lives, each in its own way, with their antennae (cut from each outline at the top of the head) swaying in the wind (`wind`): the one at the left looks around and blinks, the one in the middle rows (the oar turns in his grip and he leans into it; the rulito rides on his head), the little one hops now and then, the one on the spiral rocks to and fro humming and the sleepy one at the right end nods off and wakes with a start. Its ground is a strip of its own (`paciencia/grass.webp` and `mushrooms.webp`, from the layers in `originales/paciencia/paciencia-piso`, also from c59acb9), half as wide as the old one for its height, so it is laid three times side by side, the outer two mirrored (no seam); the roots and the spores work on the middle two strips' width, as before. The mushrooms and the little balls of the coral lean and breathe on their stems (`sway` in `useLayerStage.js`, about the caps and balls the converter found in `pacienciaScene.json`; the picture is shown in a taller box so the caps at its top have room), and when one is about to let go of its spores (`onPuff` in `useSpores.js`) it gathers itself, trembling, and pops as they come out; every 5 to 9 s one sneezes a bigger puff on its own. The pine cone is `paciencia/cone.webp` (layers 1 and 2), on the cut stem in the gap in the middle of the strip. Paciencia's sun is not a picture: all of it a stipple of live particles, as Pañña's (package S), read from the finished card by `scripts/sun-cloud.mjs` (`sun.cloud` in `pacienciaScene.json`, with the card's centre and solid radius) and laid onto the scene's own sun (`SUN`, in fractions of its old box; `sunDots()`); a disc a little smaller than its solid middle stays under it so the middle is whole, the dots covering its edge; the group follows the disc with `shift` and `grow` as it breathes. Vipassana's breath is taller than it is wide: part of the swell `useCharacterLife` gives goes into `sy` and a little comes off `sx`.

**The WebGL stage (every scene).** Since the pilot in Paciencia, the scenes are drawn whole in WebGL by `useLayerStage.js`: one canvas and one renderer per scene, every picture a plane at its depth, and the camera slides with the pointer or the tilt of the phone (`stage.look`) with its lens shifted so the focal plane stays put, which is the parallax: each part moves as far as it did in the DOM version (`amp / (SLIDE + amp)` gives its depth). Everything is laid out in px of the screen at rest, computed from the CSS of the DOM version (`layout()` or `place()` in each scene). The particles of `useStardust.js` are drawn among the layers (`{ stage, groups: [{ order }] }`), and can follow a layer (`shift` with `stage.drift(z)`) or turn with it (`turn`). Touch targets are DOM elements moved over what they belong to every frame (`boxOnScreen`, `toScreen`), so they still take a finger and the keyboard. Shader effects: `wind` (Paciencia's grass, Evolución's fronds, Pañña's leaves), `shimmer` (the air over Paciencia's sun, the organelles seen through the water, Sincronicidad's bubbles, Vipassana's rays), `water` (the light of the water across the inside of Hogar, Creatividad and Evolución, and Khanti's pink: a net of thin bright lines, `CAUSTIC`; broad soft bands were tried first and blurred the parts of the cells), `sheen` (a band of light over Khanti's glass), `sink` (Khanti's flasks empty in the shader), `blur`, `vignette`, `clip` and mirrors (`sx`/`sy`, Pañña's forest), discs and glows drawn in code. Canvases drawn by a scene (the rulito, Paciencia's roots, Creatividad's and Evolución's interiors, Khanti's thread, Pañña's floor) are shown as textures; when such a canvas changes size (a resize, a phone turned) `refresh()` makes its texture again, since a texture keeps the size it was first uploaded with. A scene builds its layers and registers `place()` before it starts the particle groups measured on them, so on a resize they are made again after the layout. The bloom pass draws into a target a quarter of the screen's size: the particle groups shrink their points to match while it runs (`onBloomScale`; drawn at full size there they came out four times too big, and on a real GPU rows of dots melted into a white smear), and the blurred light is added as light only, leaving the canvas's alpha as it was (adding alpha too darkened what was behind a see-through canvas, a scene's CSS sky: a black box round the rulito). Bloom only while `stage.bloom` is above zero: the rulito moments of Paciencia, Hogar, Dana, Pañña, Vipassana, Evolución, Khanti and Sincronicidad (and, softer, while its bubbles are lit), with the same value as `--name-glow`. Vipassana draws its field of cells with its own three.js scene and camera in the stage's renderer (`useVipassanaField.js` through `stage.underlay()`, a copy of the landing's `useCellField.js`, which stays as it was); Sid is drawn on the focal plane so his hidden element (moved by `useCharacterLife`, which hands the stage each frame with `onFrame`) is exactly where he is seen, and his glow is his own picture blurred and tinted in the shader instead of a CSS drop-shadow. Without WebGL a card shows its flat illustration (`stage()` in `cardScenes.js`). The tilt on iOS needs a permission given in answer to a touch (`useTilt.js`): on every visit the tilt is listened for and, if it does not come, the first touch asks for it (a grant iOS still holds is answered at once, with no prompt; a "no" is remembered in `localStorage` and never asked again); without it the parallax follows the finger.

**Paciencia in WebGL (the pilot).** Paciencia is drawn whole in WebGL by `useLayerStage.js`: one canvas and one renderer for the scene, every picture a plane at its depth, and the camera slides with the pointer or the tilt of the phone (`stage.look`) with its lens shifted so the focal plane stays put, which is the parallax (the sun moves 8px at the edge of a swipe, the boat 22, the ground 40; `depthFor` in the scene). The layout is the one of the DOM version, computed in `layout()` in px of the screen; on a phone the strip of coral and mushrooms is 30% bigger, so the pine cone sits higher. Shader effects: the grass sways in the wind (`wind`), the air trembles over the sun (`shimmer`), which is also a little out of focus (`blur`) and has a soft light that breathes (`glow`); the pine cone is lit by its material (`bright`, `saturate`) and a halo, not by CSS filters. The spores (`useSpores.js` with `points: true`) are particle groups of `useStardust.js` drawn by the stage between the boat and the grass, and the gold sparks in front; the roots and the rulito are their 2D canvases shown as textures. The touch target of the pine cone is a DOM element moved over it every frame (`boxOnScreen`), so it still takes a finger and the keyboard; it has `touch-action: none`, so a finger that drifts a little while holding is not taken for a scroll that lets go of the hold. When the rulito is completed the stage blooms (`stage.bloom`, the same value as `--name-glow`): the rulito, the cone, its halo, the roots and the sparks are drawn again small, blurred and added on top; it only runs while it is above zero. Without WebGL the card shows its flat illustration (`stage()` in `cardScenes.js`). The tilt on iOS needs a permission given in answer to a touch (`useTilt.js`): on every visit the tilt is listened for and, if it does not come, the first touch asks for it (a grant iOS still holds is answered at once, with no prompt; a "no" is remembered in `localStorage` and never asked again); without it the parallax follows the finger.
## Nekkhamma (0)

`SceneNekkhamma` (0) is built from the layers in `public/assets/nekkhamma` and `nekkhammaScene.json`, both made by `scripts/convert-nekkhamma.mjs` from the exported PNGs (each picture cropped to its box, in px of the 2160 square; the glowing buds each cut out on its own, `buds`, with a soft round mask; every dot of the layers that are fields of dots, as [x, y, radius, colour]: the stars, the pink specks and the cosmic cloud; and points of the whole Sid: `body`, every 11 px inside him with its colour, and `outline`, along his dark line). The sky is a CSS gradient in the card's colour; the wind strokes, the planet (its ring a separate picture; moved to the left, `PLANET`, out of the way of the cloud's galaxy), the ground (stretched to both sides of the screen) with its plants (swaying in the shader, `WIND`), the buds, the mark, and Sid are planes at their depths. Each bud breathes at its own pace (scale, brightness and a glow behind it) and sways on its stem with the same wave as the shader's wind, worked out for where it stands on the plants. The illustration draws four Sids, each more undone than the last; the scene uses only the whole one, moved to the middle (`DX`) and a little down so his feet stand in the ground (`DY`), and undoes him itself: his picture has the `dissolve` effect of `useLayerStage.js` (`uDissolve.y` 0..1: gone from the bottom up along a ragged edge of value noise a few px wide, which glows in the given colour as it eats in). He is tied by threads of dots (`threads`, a `dynamic` particle group, `threadGroup`; each a sagging, swaying curve from a point of him to one of `ANCHORS`, its dots flowing slowly towards him) to each of the seven glowing buds on the ground, his possessions. Its verb is Soltar: a touch on a bud (a DOM circle kept over it, `placeTies()`) or on him cuts a thread (`cut()`): its dots burst into motes that rise to the cloud (`release`), and he comes undone as far as the threads cut (`level` eases towards it); where each thread holds him climbs as he comes undone. As the edge passes the dots of his line (`outline`) they show where he was, as the illustration draws him undone (`lineGroup`). The cloud only exists as he lets go: the illustration's cloud, moved over him (`CLOUD`), spread sideways with two twins of each dot on his other side and thinned towards its sides and bottom, is shown in `ORDER` (from over his head outwards) as far as he is gone (`follow()`), and every other dot shown is a mote that rises to it from the edge eating him (`edgePoint()`, `flyers`, a `dynamic` group of slots; `send()`); its glow and a little spiral galaxy of particles (`useGalaxy.js`, the same as Dana's, in the colours of the cloud) come with it. The threads left are the count; left alone `HINT` seconds (7), one of them glows in a wave towards him, with its bud: that is one to cut (only one whose bud is on the screen, `seen`, measured in `placeTies()`: on a phone some are past its edges), and the ghost hand taps that bud (every third time it holds him instead). Held, the press becomes a breath after `HOLD` (`inhale`): he brightens, a glow grows behind him and motes leave him; let go after `BREATH` more every thread is cut, one quickly after the other (`exhale`, faster `rate`). With none left and nothing of his body, his line's dots rise into the cloud after `LINGER` (or at once with another touch) and he is the cloud (`dissolve()`, `converge`): it flares, then comes down, each dot back to his edge, as he is given back whole from the feet up (`bloom`), the rulito is born over his head where the card draws it (`RULITO`, `Rulito.vue` in the cyan of the drawing), the name lights up and `rulito` sounds (`sceneMilestone('renounced')`); when it goes his threads grow back slowly. Left alone `IDLE` seconds a thread grows back and he takes back a part (`back()`, `bloom`), and the cloud thins with it, another every `BACK`; whole and untouched, a few motes leave his feet now and then. Holding him does not bloom (the light behind him is enough); his threads are not in the bloom. Reduced motion keeps the scene still and not played. The planet's body is not a picture: a pink disc with its dark outline in code (`PLANET_AT`, `PLANET_PINK`, `PLANET_EDGE`); only its ring is. Sid breathes from his feet (`sy`, `sx`), and the dots of his line breathe with him.

## Dana (2)

`SceneDana` (id 2) is built from layers of the old Collaborate page (now in `public/assets/dana`, like every other card's), placed as fractions of a square frame so they keep the illustration's composition. Dana's two friends are separate layers (`public/assets/dana/left.webp`, `right.webp`, cut from the old Collaborate picture without the rulito that was drawn over the right one) set further apart than in the drawing. Its verb is Dar y recibir (give and receive), played by sharing the light: the right one (glowing, hand held out) has the little flame, which stays in his hand; a touch on anyone of the island (`GIFTS`: the other friend, the little stone creatures, the mushrooms) sends a spark of it there in an arc (`give()`, `give` sound) and lights him up (`receive()`, `link` sound, each one with his own note; the friend's glow is dots along his outline, `danaAura.json`, the others' a cloud of embers; the giver's own aura, dots along the outline of his picture, `danaGiverAura.json`, shines more with every light he gives). Giving does not make the flame smaller, it grows. When everyone has his light they all send one back at once (`giveBack()`, `converge` sound): a rulito is born over each head, the card's name lights up and `rulito` sounds (`sceneMilestone('light_shared')`); then the lights fade slowly and it can be played again. The game itself (who has a light, the lights in the air, the return and the rulitos' timing, the hint) is the verb's engine `useGiving.js`, shared with Metta and Gratitud; `give()`, `receive()`, `giveBack()` and `balance()` are its steps, and the scene answers each with its flame, auras, sparks and sounds. The hint is the ghost hand (`useGhostHand.js`): left alone `HINT_AFTER` s (7), with no light in the air, nothing coming back and no rulito (`hand.due(HINT_AFTER, wanted)`, asked by the engine through its opt-in `hint` option), one who has no light yet is picked at random (`on.cue`) and the hand taps on his touch target, twice (its two loops); he glimmers and the flame answers each time the finger presses on him (`hint.lead`: `TAP_LEAD` and one tap later; `on.hint`). Any touch sends the hand away and cancels the glimmers still to come. With reduced motion there is no hand: he only glimmers, after 7 s, as the engine does for Metta and Gratitud. With reduced motion the scene is still but the game plays the same: the lights arrive and come back at once, without flights or sparks. A still scene draws a single frame (`useParallax.js`), so the scenes with this engine call `wake()` when touched and keep drawing until the game is at rest again (`settled`). The idea is in docs/verbos-y-dinamicas.md. Dana's two friends breathe from their feet, each at its own pace, and sway a little; every `GLANCE` s (8) the one on the left turns to the one with the flame and he answers with a small nod (only a little: the flame is in his hand).

How Dana looks: a deep night sky (a gradient on the scene that darkens towards the top and ends in the card colour at the bottom, so it meets the reading), with stars in three planes of particles that shift apart with the pointer (520 faint far ones, the stars of the card, 16 big soft out-of-focus ones close to the eye that keep off the middle); the stars of the card are 640, mostly yellow and red. A small spiral galaxy turns slowly in a corner (top right on a wide screen, top left on a phone upright): `useGalaxy.js` (flat dots: a bulge, a faint disc and two logarithmic arms, the inner stars turning faster, over soft glows on its core and arms), shared with Nekkhamma's cloud. Silent shooting stars (`shooting`, two slots) cross the upper sky every few seconds. A band of dust like a nebula and two blurred islets far behind were tried and taken out. The island is drawn bigger than in the illustration, always `ISLAND` (1.22) times, grown from the line where the friends stand (`PIVOT`; its place in `LAYOUT` is worked out from those two), and `onIsland()` moves with it everything measured on it (`GIFTS` but the friend, their touch boxes, `DRIPS`, `GLINTS`); the friends keep their size. On a phone upright it fills the width and its tips are cut a little. It drips (`drops`: flat, crisp drops with a short trail, from the tips of the moss and the three lowest corners of the crystal), its crystal glints (`glints`) and fireflies wander round it (`fireflies(side)`). On a wide screen the frame is lifted off the bottom a little, so the bigger island has room for its drops.

**Dana in WebGL.** Dana is drawn by the common stage (`useLayerStage.js`, as Paciencia): `layout()` works out the square frame and the rect of every layer in px of the screen from the same percentages the DOM version used (`LAYOUT`, `RULITOS`), and the island, the friends, the two rulitos (their canvases as textures) and the flame hang from one group (`L.isle`) at one depth, which floats; the camera slides 14px with the pointer or the tilt (`SLIDE`, `depthFor`). The particles are groups of `useStardust.js` drawn by the stage in order: the sky (far stars, galaxy, shooting stars, the stars of the card) before every layer, each friend's aura just behind him, half the fireflies behind the right one, the flame's embers behind its ball, and in front of everything what is given (the glows of the little ones, the lights in the air, the sparks), the drops, the glints, the other fireflies and the out-of-focus stars. The groups that follow the island are measured in % of the frame and carried by an affine map of the group taken once a frame (`measure()`, `onCanvas()`), not one projection per particle. The touch targets are DOM elements moved over the island every frame (`placeHits()`, `boxOnScreen`, at least 44px). When the rulitos are whole the stage blooms with the card's name (`stage.bloom`): the rulitos, the flame, its embers, the lights in the air and the sparks.

## Sincronicidad (29)

`SceneSincronicidad` (29) is built from the layers in `public/assets/sincronicidad` and `sincronicidadScene.json`, both made by `scripts/convert-sincronicidad.mjs` from the exported PNGs (`originales/sincronicidad`, 3240 px, scaled to the 2160 square; in the git history). The sky has no layer and is not a picture: it is the card's flat night (`#36848e`, a little deeper at the top), painted in CSS, with the land's teal at the very bottom. Its stars are every dot of the card's two star layers (pink and yellow) as particles; past the frame the square of sky is repeated, mirrored, so the stars go on wherever the screen does. The card's one long stroke among them is left out and silent shooting stars are drawn in code instead (`shooting`). The soft salmon light inside each bubble is not a picture either: the converter fits the ellipse of its solid middle (a `disc()`, which warms towards yellow on the bubble's beat) and turns its stippled rim into live particles; each rim is its own group that follows its bubble's float (`grow`, `turn`, `shift`) and flares on the beat. The pictures are the land (the ground, its stones and the hearth), the drawn flame (it sways, `wind`, with the air trembling over it, `shimmer`, and its living tongues of `useFlame` rise just behind it, under a warm `glow()` that breathes and leaps), and every bubble's skin (teal rims and pink, `shimmer`) apart from its larvae. The larvae breathe from their feet: those in the eggs turn slowly, the two of the meeting turn to each other every `LOOK` s (9), and the pair hold each other closer as they breathe. On a wide screen the land goes on past the frame: copies of its left part (`CROP`, which has no hearth), mirrored one after the other, and on the right lifted by the difference between the ground's two edges. On a phone upright the frame is as big as the four bubbles and the fire allow across (`SPAN`, `FOCUS`), and never so big that the smallest egg goes under the card's name (`titleBottom`). The touch targets are at least 48 px.

Its verb is Resonar, on time: a glow runs along the four bubbles on a steady beat (`BEAT` 1.3 s; the fire on the fifth beat, a rest on the sixth, `PERIOD`), each brightest and throwing sparks on its beat. A touch within `IN_TIME` (0.28 s) of a bubble's beat makes it resonate (`resonate`, its note of the chord climbing from the egg to the pair) and it stays lit, with a glow behind it at its depth (`L.halos`, bloom); a touch out of time sounds muted (`miss`) and that bubble waits for its next beat (`missed`), so tapping again and again does not catch it. While someone plays the glow sounds softly on every bubble (`pulse`). Layers in `public/assets/esperanza` and `esperanzaScene.json` (made by `scripts/convert-esperanza.mjs` from `originales/esperanza`, 3240 px, scaled to 2160). The sky is in no layer: its flat teal is read from the finished card (`public/cards/30.webp`, where nothing is drawn) and painted in code, with a soft light round the moon (`glow()`). Every star is a particle: the card's own, and as many again over the sky the frame does not cover. The stipple of the moon's shadow and the being's pink aura are particles too. Pictures: the moon (its white and line work), the land (sands, shadows, stones and every line), the two ginkgos, the being, and the grass and mushrooms cut into one atlas (`tufts.webp`, with the land's lines cut out of them so the outlines show through) so each tuft sways on its own (`wind`). Beyond the frame the land goes on mirrored, as `sides.webp`: the sands without the stones (no stone is seen twice), with the dune outline drawn again whole where the stones broke it; the tufts are mirrored onto it except those near the edge, and one tall tuft hides the V where the right-hand dune meets its mirror. The moon breathes in its light and bobs; sand grains blow low over the dunes. The being breathes (its curled tail swells, `sway`), now and then lifts its head to the moon and otherwise looks about, and its aura breathes with it. A touch strums the chord (`star`) and the stars flare; a touch (or Enter) on the being makes it look up and its aura flare. The card's name is written in the sky's own teal, so the scene sets `--name-glow` to 0.8 for it to read. Not converted: 22 (the Algo Particular mark alone in the sky) and the little yellow squiggle among the stars (taken as the rulito; only its box is kept as `rulito`). Reduced motion keeps it still (and without the game). Its verb is Ver, with Pañña's threads (`useThreads.js`): among the stars, the bright ones of an ancestor (a soft halo each; its own in gold and two paler decoys) are joined by dragging from one to another; the ancestor is a constellation in the shape of a being like the little one, and there are three, always in order (`FIGURES`: sitting, 7 threads; walking, 6; resting, 6). They are drawn in the open sky over the moon on a phone and to the right of the moon on a wide screen, never under the card's name. The right threads are firm and the others faint; after 7 s alone the ancestor draws itself, faint; at the last thread it is judged (`unravel` if not). Seen (`converge`, milestone `pattern_seen`), it shines, then gathers itself, shrinks and goes to stand behind the little one (its `rest` spot) and lets a thread of light down to its head: it looks up and its aura grows. With the three, their threads flow fast together (`converge`), its aura opens (`open`) and the rulito is born over its head (milestone `ancestors_gathered`); when it goes, the ancestors go back into the stars. The threads, the bright stars and the ancestors are one group of particles that moves with the stars (`sky`), with the glow under the threads and a group of halos.

## Metta (8)

`SceneMetta` (8) is built from the layers in `public/assets/metta` and `mettaScene.json`, both made by `scripts/convert-metta.mjs` from the exported PNGs (`originales/metta`, in the git history). The night is not a picture: its navy is in no layer, so the converter reads it from the finished card (`public/cards/8.webp`, only the pixels that are sky) into stops for a CSS gradient. The two lights are not pictures either: the great yellow one (2) and the turquoise one round Sid (4, with its stipple 3) are discs in code (`disc()`), each with a soft glow, and their stippled bands are live particles laid over the disc's edge, so no circle shows (package S, as Pañña's sun). They are too big to be all dots, so only the band is: its profile, by distance from the centre, is read from the finished card by `scripts/sun-cloud.mjs` on the arcs where nothing is drawn over it (the card's stars, squiggles and rulito left out) and drawn all round from it (`halo.cloud`, `aura.cloud`); each disc is only as big as where its band is still whole (`cloud.solid`), so the band's densest dots cover its edge; `stippleCloud.js` turns them into dots. The yellow stars (1), the white stars inside the turquoise light (5), the stipple of the five glowing flowers' lights (8, each with a breathing `glow()`) and the spores over the little mushrooms (8, rising and fading, a dynamic group) are particles too. The three little squiggles among the stars are cut into an atlas (`bits.webp`) with every being's eyes and Sid's. The hill is drawn in code on a canvas (`paintHill()`): the top of its flat colour (6) is read every 8 px; inside the frame the line along its top (7) is a picture, and past the frame on wide screens the hill goes on sloping down as it does at the edges, with the line continued in code, so there are no mirrored twins or seams. The ground (stones, little mushrooms and tufts) and each glowing flower (cut out of 9, swaying with `wind`) are pictures; so are the six beings (each with its fills, line and arm), the fox, the kitten, Sid (59–63) and the nest with his round body in it (65–70). The frame rests on the bottom of the screen; on a wide screen `DROP` (0.08) of it goes under the edge, and on a phone upright it is as big as `SPAN` (1720 art px across, centred) allows. Depths: stars, yellow light, turquoise light, hill, beings, Sid and nest, fox and kitten. The sky is as our skies always are (Dana's): a far plane of tiny faint stars (moving a little with the pointer), the card's stars a little bigger than drawn, in the colours of our skies (mostly yellow, some cream and coral), twinkling and stirred by a finger, two silent shooting stars now and then (a cream head and a yellow tail) and a few big out-of-focus stars close to the eye. The turquoise light is a small sky of its own: a far plane of tiny stars kept inside it, the card's white stars twinkling and stirred by a finger, and now and then a small shooting star that fades before its edge.

Everyone breathes from their feet at their own pace, blinks now and then and glances about (often at Sid), and each has a way of its own, as the crew of Paciencia (`way()`): the one standing at the left rocks from foot to foot; the one with crossed arms nods to Sid now and then; the one sitting cross-legged nods off little by little and wakes with a start; the two arm in arm sway together, one a moment after the other, and now and then lean in; the one sitting at the right hums, rocking to and fro; the fox sniffs the air (two quick lifts of the nose) and its tail sways; the kitten hops now and then (crouch, jump, soft landing) and its tail sways (`sway`). The squiggles float and the turquoise light breathes with Sid.

Its verb is Dar y recibir, with Dana's engine shared in `useGiving.js`, and the card says where it starts: *"comenzando por vos mismo"*, *"no podemos dar lo que no tenemos"*. Sid's own light is low at first. The first touch has to be on him: he loves himself (`give` and `link` on his own note), his turquoise light swells and his aura of dots opens, and everyone looks at him. Only then can he give: a touch on anyone round him (a button each, also with the keyboard) sends a light from his heart to them in an arc with a trail of sparks; they light up (a cloud of turquoise, yellow, white and pink dots round them), hop, look at Sid and sound his note and then theirs (`link`, left low, right high). Touching another before him is not a mistake: that one looks at Sid and his light calls (`tap`). With the eight lit they all send a light back to him at once (`converge`): his light swells, the rulito is born over his head (pink, as the one drawn on the card, a little bigger), the card's name lights up and `rulito` sounds; milestone `light_shared` with `times`. Then the lights fade and he is back to the start: to give again, he loves himself first. Left alone 7 s, Sid's light calls if he has not loved himself yet; otherwise one who has no light glimmers. A touch anywhere else strums the chord (`star`) and the stars and the lights' stipple flare. Reduced motion keeps it still; the game still plays, without flights or sparks. Not converted: 10 (the Algo Particular mark, alone on the ground) and 71 (the rulito over Sid; its box is kept in the JSON and the game draws its own there).
